Finding: The two cases were six days apart. They do not establish whether technology changes the speed of regime collapse.

Do political hawks kill political doves?

Linguistics · 19 Nov 2026

Finding: Bird imagery does extend to feathers, calls and flight. Killing or eating political doves was not found in this check.

What made decipherment possible, and could AI help?

Linguistics · 19 Nov 2026

Finding: Readable parallels, names or related languages were key in this sample. An AI agent advantage has not been demonstrated.

What can canals and nuclear power tell us about AI investment?

Economic history · 19 Nov 2026

Finding: These were smaller investment booms. Nuclear revenue was substantial at operating plants; revenue alone does not show profitability.

Is the AI investment boom unprecedented?

Economic history · 19 Nov 2026

Finding: US railways offer a close precedent. Optimism about AI returns still depends on asset life, costs and future demand.

Did work change more before 1970 than since?

Economic history · 17 Nov 2026

Finding: It depends on how jobs are counted. Modern categories and wider social measures show as much change, or more, in the later period.

Can a cake be sickly?

Linguistics · 17 Nov 2026

Finding: Both “a sickly cake” and “the cake is sickly” occur. The American sample cannot establish a British–American difference.

Could AI control more wealth by 2035 than humans do today?

Economic history · 17 Nov 2026

Finding: Plausible as an aggressive scenario for delegated investment decisions. History does not establish a global wealth crossover.

Is only one of 270 jobs from 1950 obsolete?

Economic history · 16 Nov 2026

Finding: The original count concerns jobs eliminated by automation. At least 15 more have gone; “obsolete” is a different claim.
