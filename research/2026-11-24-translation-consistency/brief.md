# Author decisions and open interview

## Confirmed scope

Scoping only. Investigate the feasibility of neighbouring-language questionnaire comparison, corpus-assisted interpretation in more distant languages, consistency across iterations, and an education-survey extension. No build or publication. No new subagents. No new WVS registration or downloads. Existing restricted questionnaire and response files remain outside the public repository. Short item quotations in a write-up are permitted by the commission; that is distinct from a provider licence clause.

The exact first-study items, languages, methods and full brief remain open. This confirms the series direction, not every detail of the agent's proposed pilot.

## Potential investigations in the series

1. **Neighbouring-language questionnaire key words come first.** Investigate whether candidate equivalents carry comparable associations in ordinary use, through linguistic judgement with corpus support. Whole questions supply the context for word selection; the first snack is not a whole-question equivalence audit or response-effect study. The exact languages and word sample still need agreement.
2. **Corpus collocations in more distant languages remain a proposed extension.** Investigate whether candidate equivalents carry comparable associations and uses.
3. **Consistency across survey iterations remains a proposed extension.** Separate translation changes from source-item changes and examine any response shifts descriptively.
4. **TIMSS questionnaire translations are a confirmed topic of interest.** PISA, ESS and ISSP remain possible comparison sources rather than substitutes already selected by the author.
5. **Official EU translations are a new potential investigation requested on 24 November.** Compare how selected terms or expressions are officially rendered in different languages and how the renderings differ. A possible method is to align the same passage and document version across languages, then compare institutional usage with ordinary corpus usage. Specific institutions, document types, terms, languages, sources and access/licensing have not been selected or checked. Record this as a future candidate, not an authorised research run.

## Focus question answered through the coordinator

What should the first neighbouring-language snack mainly establish?

- A. Whether the whole questionnaire items ask the same thing, using corpus evidence where useful. This is the agent's recommendation because it directly tests comparability.
- B. Whether selected key words carry the same associations in ordinary usage. This would make the first snack primarily a lexical/corpus investigation.
- C. Whether a few suspected wording differences coincide with different response patterns. This would add descriptive response analysis and require more control of survey-design differences.

Answer relayed at 08:08 UK time on 24 November 2026 (Tuesday): the author chose key words and their associations in ordinary use, mainly corpus, with equivalent words assessed through judgement and corpus support. This supersedes the agent's whole-item-first recommendation. It does not ask for response analysis or authorise a build.

## Next sample proposal

The [language, word and corpus proposal](proposal-neighbouring-language-keywords.md) recommends Czech, Slovak and Polish, using the EVS 2017 child-quality and social-duty items to select three lexical groups: industriousness/hard work, responsibility and duty/obligation. Short forms were checked on selected source pages; the source PDFs were not copied to the public package. Czech WVS `tvrdá práce` supplies an additional lexical contrast, without adding EVS/WVS response analysis. English glosses orient the reader; English master wording remains to be checked before claiming source-target equivalence.

Recommended main corpora: Czech Web 2023 `preloaded/cstenten23_mj2`, Slovak Web 2023 `preloaded/sktenten23_rft1` and Polish Web 2019 `preloaded/pltenten19_rft3`. Each exact metadata request succeeded. No collocation, word-sketch or concordance query was run. ## Writing declaration

The scoping report is FOR DOMINIK. Its purpose is to support choosing a first study, not to establish translation errors. The reader knows the Czech culture check raised wording concerns; they need accessible sources, honest conclusions, limits and feasible alternatives. Private culture-guide content, account details, personal paths and research respondent data are irrelevant and excluded. The genre requires a clear recommendation, source map, proposed study shapes, unresolved questions and measured usage coverage.

The domain map supplies terms and risks. The source/access section establishes feasibility. The study-shape section explains what each option answers and cannot answer. The decision section asks for scope only; it does not turn a provisional recommendation into author approval.
