# Author decisions and open interview

Scoping only. Investigate the feasibility of neighbouring-language questionnaire comparison, corpus-assisted interpretation in more distant languages, consistency across iterations, and an education-survey extension. ## Writing declaration

The scoping report is FOR DOMINIK. Its purpose is to support choosing a first study, not to establish translation errors. The reader knows the Czech culture check raised wording concerns; they need accessible sources, honest conclusions, limits and feasible alternatives. Private culture-guide content, account details, personal paths and research respondent data are irrelevant and excluded. The genre requires a clear recommendation, source map, proposed study shapes, unresolved questions and measured usage coverage.

The domain map supplies terms and risks. The source/access section establishes feasibility. The study-shape section explains what each option answers and cannot answer. The decision section asks for scope only; it does not turn a provisional recommendation into author approval.
