# SciNet and the Research Snacks run records

**Purpose and reader.** The approval brief gives Dominik a decision about a small retrospective comparison. He chose the question on Tuesday 24 November 2026 and can judge the linguistics matches. He needs the unit being compared, what can be recovered, the risks to interpretation and the cost. The approval page summarises the decision. This map and the inventory carry the source and coverage detail. Deployment and publication decisions are outside this brief.

**Author decision.** Compare the snacks' own runs with SciNet's ratings. [Four SciNet ideas](../2026-11-24-scinet-ideas/ideas.md). The cohort, rules and time limits are proposals.

**Terms and positions.** A run event is an observed attempt with one aim, an output or explicit failure, and a recorded actor or recording limit. A SciNet task is a typical unit of team research with an action, object, purpose and substeps. The two units can differ. SciNet's Elo is a model judge's relative estimate of Opus 5's chance of completing the digital work to a competent standard with supplied access and no human correction. An audit of these retrospective run records can ask whether documented outcomes follow that order. A performance test would require independently graded attempts with the same model, tools and tasks. [SciNet methodology](data/METHODOLOGY.md), [Elo documentation](data/TASK_DIMENSIONS.md#elo-ratings-for-claude-opus-5).

**Methods and data.** A candidate match needs a shared action, evidence object, intended result, and comparable scope across relevant substeps. A match to a single substep is descriptive. Compare archived attempt evidence and material corrections separately from tool access, new instructions and presentation changes. The [evidence inventory](evidence-scinet-runs.md) records the first pass. The release's validation compares importance, prevalence and time estimates with external material. Its Elo consistency checks compare model judgements, with 70% agreement on 1,958 shared comparisons when a second model rejudged a subset. No actual Opus 5 task attempts were run for the rating.

**Pitfalls and gaps.** Published pages select what to narrate. A token table measures usage, a rebuild measures reproducibility of computation, and a saved label proves a judgement was recorded. Independent evidence is needed to establish competence or that a substantive fix was necessary. Some fixes come from another agent, and some author interventions change the brief. Corpus construction and annotation are common places where the observed work covers only one piece of a rated task. Unmatched and unclear cases belong in the denominator for coverage.
