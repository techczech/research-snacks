# Independent option scores

Two independent sessions used the same inherited model configuration. Their resolved model identities and token usage were not reported by the native tool; the judges' own identity statements are not treated as verification. This is a same-configuration panel, not confirmation from different model families.

Both judges received the [same frozen options and rubric](packet-professional-qualification-options.md), without the report's recommendation or the other judge's answer. They scored fit to the question, warranted inference and feasibility of a first preview with equal weight. Both passed the simple calibration comparing a warranted descriptive route claim with an unwarranted universal causal claim. That calibration does not validate the source audit.

| Option | Fit to the question, each judge | Warranted inference, each judge | First-preview feasibility, each judge | Mean overall / 10 | Overall range |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| A. Qualification timeline | 9 | 8 | 5 | 7.33 | 7.33–7.33 |
| B. Medicine evidence comparison | 7 | 8 | 8 | 7.67 | 7.67–7.67 |
| C. Education explorer | 4 | 8 | 8 | 6.67 | 6.67–6.67 |

The judges gave identical scores. Their zero spread does not measure factual reliability, and the small difference between A and B should not be treated as a precise quality estimate. Both preferred B because it directly addresses outcomes with a smaller first preview. Both identified the same strongest objection: B could replace the requested international comparison across professions and Asian systems with a narrow medical-education review. If that breadth is essential to the first output, both would favour A.

The researching agent recommends A because preserving that breadth has greater priority in their interpretation of the request. That is a preference about scope, distinct from the judges' equal-weight ranking. The author chooses whether breadth or a faster, more direct outcome test should lead.

These are packet-based judgements. Neither scoring session independently inspected original data, route rules or full papers. Raw scores and criterion reasons remain in [judge one](judge-professional-qualification-one.json), [judge two](judge-professional-qualification-two.json) and the [scoring run record](run-professional-qualification-scoring.json).
