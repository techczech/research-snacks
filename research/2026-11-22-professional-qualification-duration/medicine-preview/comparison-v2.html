What is being compared

First registration can permit supervised work only. Full registration can precede completion of residency. Independent general practice, a GP specialty credential and permission to manage a clinic are different endpoints. Markers show conditional eligibility on a continuous successful route, not observed issue dates or processing times. Markers with the same shape indicate a category, not identical legal rights.

How the clocks were built

The bars show selected full-time pathways. They do not show average completion times, every available route or the effect of training length on competence.

School structures come from selected UNESCO UIS workbook rows, mostly 2020–21, with World Bank theoretical-entry-age indicators from 2025. Bavaria uses its current nine-year Gymnasium. Singapore includes junior college, which UIS classifies after secondary education. The model joins these structures to rules checked on 22 November 2026; it does not follow a single historical cohort. Compulsory schooling and the route to university are kept separate.

Calculations assume continuous full-time study, successful examinations and no admission gaps. Service bonds, Singapore National Service, part-time training and individual recognition decisions can extend elapsed time. The main later-training bars all concern general practice or family medicine. The credentials and registration rights still differ; completing a training period is not automatically the award of a qualification, accreditation or permission to run a practice.

Variation in route length does not establish that shorter routes are equally effective, or that longer routes are necessary. This preview maps requirements. It does not estimate a causal effect on competence.

Data, coding and reproduction files

All stages and sources
California, US — selected route and limits

Conventional US MD → family medicine residency

Four-year college and four-year MD are conventional route assumptions, not a California statutory minimum. US and Canadian graduates need 12 months of approved postgraduate training; international graduates have a different rule. The family medicine residency totals three years including its first year. Initial licence renewal requires 36 months or continued enrolment, followed by later verification. Board certification is a specialty credential, not a universal legal condition for a general medical licence.

Core licence and residency requirements read; conventional degree duration from indexed AAMC guidance

England — selected route and limits

Standard undergraduate medicine → Foundation → GP training

Five-year degree is the selected standard route; four-year graduate-entry courses need prior education. Full registration after Foundation year 1 does not qualify a doctor for independent NHS general practice. The three-year GP programme is a later specialty route. CCT, GP Register and applicable NHS performers-list requirements remain conditions, rather than zero-time guarantees.

GMC route, curriculum and NHS programme guidance read

Bavaria, Germany — selected route and limits

Medical state examination → Approbation → Allgemeinmedizin

Six years three months is the statutory standard course including the final examination period, not an observed average or an invariant earliest possible completion. The 48-week practical year is embedded and must not be added again. Approbation is the first general medical licence. The GP specialist qualification requires five more years and examination; statutory-insurance participation and setting up a practice have further conditions.

Federal sections and Bavarian specialty duration read

Japan — selected route and limits

Medicine → initial clinical training → general practice specialty

The licence follows six-year medical education and the national examination. Clinical practice then requires at least two years of initial clinical training. Completion is not itself the opening of an insured clinic. For people who had not completed initial training by 1 April 2026, the standard facility-manager route adds three years of insured hospital practice; alternative and transitional rules exist. The main later-training route is the Japanese Medical Specialty Board general practice (総合診療) specialty, requiring at least three years. Board examination and certification remain additional conditions.

Current Japanese Act licensing clauses, ministry route diagram and relevant JMSB general-practice standards read

India — selected route and limits

MBBS → internship → optional MD Family Medicine

The academic phase is 54 months and the internship twelve months. Passing final academic examinations permits provisional internship registration, not independent practice. Permanent registration follows internship and other eligibility conditions. The optional MD Family Medicine route is three years after MBBS/internship for a candidate without an existing relevant postgraduate diploma. An NMC permission letter for CMC Vellore confirms that the regulator authorises this qualification; access to a place is not assumed universal. NExT was notified and deferred; it is not modelled as an operating extra examination year. The original NMC curriculum, internship regulation and deferral notice were retrieved. No later NExT commencement notice was found in the current rules index and bounded news search on 22 November 2026; rules applying to a future cohort can change.

Original NMC curriculum, internship and postgraduate regulations and deferral notice read in relevant sections; legacy provisional-registration provision corroborated by government hospital practice

Singapore — selected route and limits

NTU MBBS → PGY1 → family medicine residency

The five-year NTU degree is an explicitly selected local route; Duke-NUS graduate-entry medicine has prior-degree requirements. Provisional registration covers twelve months of PGY1. Eligible local graduates may apply for full registration after satisfactory PGY1; a practising certificate is also needed. Full registration permits independent practice; family-physician accreditation is a separate credential. The main later-training bar is the three-year family-medicine residency. Completion is distinct from Family Physician accreditation, which also requires a relevant qualification, recognised FM practice experience and currency of practice; relevant residency postings may count. A new family-medicine specialist route is planned for 2028 and is not applied retrospectively. National Service and admission gaps are not included in the academic clock.
