# American evidence limits

The available written OANC supplies twelve exact-form tokens: eleven manually read as attributive and one predicative about children. Speech supplies one attributive token about kids. There is no clear food evaluation in this thirteen-token sample. This does not establish a national prohibition or an attributive-only food construction.

All twelve written tokens were tagged RB in the retrieved corpus version despite clear adjective contexts. The empty automatic word sketch therefore cannot be treated as a linguistic absence. Word-form concordance retrieval avoided the POS gate.
