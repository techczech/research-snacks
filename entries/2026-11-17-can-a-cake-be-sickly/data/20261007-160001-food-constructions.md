# Food constructions

The BNC selected food annotation contains two sole and three coordinated attributive cases, three sole and two coordinated explicit verbal complements, and four sole and one coordinated elliptical/supplementary case. This is fifteen selected examples, not an exhaustive BNC census. Adjacent sickly sweet/rich is a different construction.

The enTenTen13 cake passage supplies both attributive and predicative sickly for the same cake, without coordinated adjectives. Its later predicate is recoverable in the context of the archived anchor at token 20007415499. The token position is not asserted to be the later predicate's own offset.
