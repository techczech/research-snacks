## Corpus evidence

Live Sketch Engine queries on 7 October 2026 retrieved all 218 BNC exact-form hits, all 12 written OANC hits and the single spoken OANC hit. Completed query indicators and token positions are in the archived JSON. Wider contexts were inspected for the selected predicative examples. Counts are exact-form counts, not adjective-only or lemma-wide counts. American syntax was manually classified: eleven attributive tokens and one predicative token in writing; one attributive token in speech. No full BNC syntactic annotation was produced.

The primary source for each linguistic observation is the archived corpus context. Corpus composition, sparse American evidence, quoted authors and incorrect American POS tags limit dialect inference. BNC noun-modification and copular word-sketch relations are not complete frequency counts.

## Food-construction follow-up on 7 October 2026

Additional corpus queries used ukWaC and enTenTen13. The completed direct enTenTen13 food query returned 28 candidate rows, all inspected; plant, colour, figurative, tagging and spam candidates were not treated as literal-food evidence. The matched cake construction occurs in the same archived passage at token 20007415499. The direct cookie/biscuit query returned zero completed hits; the modified-adjective query returned two completed candidates, one literal biscuit and one cosmetic-smell comparison. The BNC food annotation records fifteen manually selected direct-adjective examples, including gapped and supplementary predication and three conversational fragments. It does not claim a complete statistical model of construction preference.

Initial ukWaC requests specified doc.url, an unavailable field, and returned empty references. Corrected requests using doc.website and doc.id are archived alongside the initial payloads. The ukWaC direct candidate query returned 111 rows, most containing adjectival sweet rather than a food noun; all five rows with other immediate candidate lemmas were inspected. The narrow ukWaC predicate query returned four completed candidates, of which two predicate sickly directly and two predicate sickly sweet.

Requests that timed out: broad attributive and predicative food queries in enTenTen21; a cookies/biscuits sentence-window query in enTenTen21; a broader sentence-window query in enTenTen13; and the narrow cookie/biscuit and other-food predicate queries in enTenTen13. These requests produced no usable payload and no zero-hit inference. The completed corpus payloads are primary evidence, with source URLs retained as corpus references rather than assertions that the live URLs remain accessible. No newer nationally balanced UK/US dataset was added.

## Shareable report and association statistics

The later report request added completed collx queries for BNC and OANC writing, using lemma collocates at -5 through +5, candidate frequency >=5, pair count >=2, cmaxitems=100, and t/m/d measures sorted by d. Every returned MI, T-score and logDice value was recalculated from the returned counts with token denominators 112338376 and 13572382. All matched within 0.00002 of the engine's rounded values. BNC returned the cap of 100 candidates; self sickly was omitted from the 99-row display. OANC returned thirteen rows. Full responses are archived. No lexical independence or significance claim is made.
